[2027 Winter Camp] Early-Bird Discount — Ends October 31

2026.08.20

The 2027 Winter Camp early-bird event has been extended to 10/31.
Apply by 10/31 to receive a discount of up to USD 390 depending on program length; regular prices will apply from 11/1.
Limited to the first 30 applicants, confirmed in order of deposit payment.

■ Schedule (Angeles Campus)
4 weeks, Session 1 — 01/03/2027 (Sun) – 01/30 (Sat)
4 weeks, Session 2 — 01/17/2027 (Sun) – 02/13 (Sat)
6 weeks — 01/03/2027 (Sun) – 02/13 (Sat)
8 weeks — 01/03/2027 (Sun) – 02/27 (Sat)

■ Class Structure
7 periods a day, 1 period = 50 minutes.
Classes are divided into 4 periods of 1:1 lessons and 3 periods of group lessons, and group classes have a maximum of 6 students.
In the evening, students memorize the vocabulary they learned that day.

■ Early-Bird Prices (for 1 person · US dollars · round-trip airfare not included · regular price → early bird)
4 weeks, Sessions 1 & 2
4-person room USD 3,060 → USD 2,830 (USD 230 off)
2-person room USD 3,290 → USD 3,060 (USD 230 off)

6 weeks
4-person room USD 4,360 → USD 4,050 (USD 310 off)
2-person room USD 4,700 → USD 4,390 (USD 310 off)

8 weeks
4-person room USD 5,350 → USD 4,970 (USD 380 off)
2-person room USD 5,810 → USD 5,420 (USD 390 off)

The early-bird event ends on 10/31/2026, and regular prices will apply from 11/1.

■ Included
Registration fee, English tuition, daily classes, weekend study fee, textbooks, accommodation, meals, weekday evening snacks, laundry and cleaning, exchange with a local school (Dalis), airport pickup and send-off, notarization paperwork service for students traveling without a parent, chaperone teacher accompaniment, certificate of completion, travel insurance

■ Not Included
Round-trip airfare, weekend activities (announced separately), SSP issuance fee (actual cost), stay extension fee (programs over 4 weeks, actual cost), notarization fee for the parental consent form, Philippine entry fee ₱3,620 (subject to exchange rates), spending money, math and English grammar textbooks, airline UM (Unaccompanied Minor) service fee (when departing individually), 2-night, 3-day long-distance trip cost
The notarization fee and entry fee apply when a minor under the age of 15 enters the Philippines without a parent.

■ Payment
Deposit USD 800; the balance is due 4 weeks before departure.

Prices shown are in US dollars and may be adjusted according to exchange rate fluctuations.
