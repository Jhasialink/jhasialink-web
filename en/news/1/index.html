[Angeles Campus] Second-Half Event

2026.08.20

Second-Half Event Information

This schedule is for those who would like to study with us while avoiding the peak vacation season.
It applies to programs starting on any date you choose between 8/24 and 12/25/2026, and is limited to the first 20 applicants.

■ Class Structure
6 periods a day, 1 period = 50 minutes.
Of these, 4 periods are 1:1 lessons and 2 periods are group lessons, for a total of 300 minutes a day.
Group classes have a maximum of 6 students.

■ Fees (for 1 person · US dollars · round-trip airfare not included)
4 weeks  — 4-person room USD 1,450 / 2-person room USD 1,680
6 weeks  — 4-person room USD 2,180 / 2-person room USD 2,520
8 weeks  — 4-person room USD 2,900 / 2-person room USD 3,360
12 weeks — 4-person room USD 4,120 / 2-person room USD 4,810

The 12-week price reflects a long-term enrollment discount.

■ Included
Tuition, dormitory fee, meals (lunch and dinner, Mon–Fri), textbook fee, management fee, airport pickup and drop-off, travel insurance

■ Not Included
Round-trip airfare, personal spending money, notarization fee for minors, Philippine entry fee ₱3,620 (subject to exchange rates)
The notarization fee and entry fee apply when a minor under the age of 15 enters the Philippines without a parent.

■ Payment
Deposit USD 800; the balance is due one week before arrival.

The event may close early depending on the number of applicants.

Prices shown are in US dollars and may be adjusted according to exchange rate fluctuations.

Inquiries: +82-10-6886-7860 (Yun, Manager)
