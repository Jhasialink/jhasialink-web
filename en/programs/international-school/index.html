Local International School Course
From international school admission to daily-life management,
JH ASIA LINK handles the course that prepares students before they adapt locally.
From learning to daily life, all in one
A program in which students enroll in an international school in Angeles and take classes in a real English environment, while JH Asia Link takes care of room and board, study, and daily life 24 hours a day.
Practical adjustment before studying in the US or Canada
Students take classes in English at a local international school and experience practical English and daily life before studying abroad. An ideal preparatory course before full study abroad in the US or Canada.
Partner schools: Westfields / Noblesse · Cambridge CAIE + IB
4-Step Management System
01
Admission Prep
