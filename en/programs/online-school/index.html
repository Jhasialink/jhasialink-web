What is Yeil International Christian School?
An accredited school where students complete an official US curriculum online from outside the US, rather than attending classes in the US. The diploma is equivalent to those from US public and private schools.

Flexible Learning Structure
Students can enroll full-time or take individual courses. They can start whenever they choose and study without limits on place or time. The program adapts to a wide range of schedules and learning styles.
US-Accredited Education
Students who meet the graduation requirements receive a Yeil International Christian School diploma. This diploma is recognized as equivalent to a diploma from a US public or private high school and can be used to apply to US colleges.
Multimedia Coursework
The official curriculum consists of virtual labs, animated tutorials, and online lectures. It includes individual feedback from teachers and graded assignments, and students can get help from teachers at any time.

CARE SYSTEM — Why it's more than just taking classes
Many students move on after an online class without fully understanding the material. Asia Link checks each student's understanding right after class and fills in any gaps that same day.
STEP 1
Take Online Classes
Take Yeil's official classes — including multimedia lectures, virtual labs, and assignments.
STEP 2
Understanding Check
Right after class, JH's dedicated teachers immediately check each student's understanding of the key content.
STEP 3
Individual Support for Weak Areas
Anything not understood is retaught 1:1 — we never simply move on.
STEP 4
English Expression Correction + Advanced Work
Along with understanding the subject content, students' English expressions, vocabulary, and sentence structure are corrected at the same time.
STEP 5
Individual Feedback & Parent Reports
Learning progress and comprehension are recorded and shared with parents in regular reports.
