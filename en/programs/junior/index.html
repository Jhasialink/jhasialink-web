Activity List
Make fun memories and gain new experiences at various attractions around Clark.
01
Clark Safari
A zoo in Clark. A unique program to see giraffes, lions, and other wild animals up close.
02
Clark Aqua Planet
The largest water park in the Philippines. Enjoy various attractions plus dolphin and seal shows.
03
Subic Ocean Adventure
A unique program with a large dolphin show, seal and sea lion shows, and interaction with sea creatures.
04
Clark El Kabayo
A horseback riding facility in Clark.
A unique riding activity in a Western-style setting.
05
Angeles Forest Cafe
An outdoor cafe with a pool.
A healing spot to relax amid lush nature.
06
Clark Sandbox
An adventure park with various outdoor activities such as ziplines and rope courses.
07
SM City Clark
Clark's largest shopping and dining complex.
Ideal for shopping and enjoying food during free time.
08
Alpha Aviation Clark
A flight simulator experience at Clark Airport.
Get a vivid feel for being a real pilot.
In addition to these 8, various other activity programs are offered, such as a dinosaur theme park.
!
Note
•Schedules and activities may change depending on local conditions.
•With safety as the top priority, professional chaperone teachers accompany students.
