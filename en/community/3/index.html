2026 Summer Camp: A Parent's Review (Y.U.)

2026.08.20

My child is cheerful and lively but not comfortable being in front of others, and was a bit lacking in presentation skills and confidence. Seeing my child settle in faster than anyone and grow stronger day by day, I felt the teachers had truly drawn out my child's potential. My child said the first week was really hard to get used to. But in the second and third weeks it became a lot of fun, and being able to express what they wanted to say, both in speech and in writing, made them very happy. They say they want to go again the year after next with their younger sibling. Making small talk with the teachers during breaks and spending time with the older girls they met there all seem to have become good memories.

"How much can they improve in one month? Well, it'll be a good experience anyway" — that's how I felt sending my child to camp... and yes, my child really improved in one month. Speaking, listening, reading, and writing, all of them! Above all, I think confidence grew the most. We're traveling abroad as a family next month, and my child is now in charge of every situation that needs English. Full of confidence 🤣👍

Various activities every week, water play after class, DJ time every night, delicious barbecue and fruit parties... Thank you, JH Asia Link, for such a varied and fun experience 😍
